An agency on paper.

Exortium does not exist. This site is a design concept: how a three-person marketing agency could introduce itself if it were allowed to be loud.

{{reg}}

The idea.

Small firms often buy marketing in pieces: a website from one place, adverts from another, and nobody answering the enquiries. The agency imagined here sells the three as one job with one person to ring.

The page is laid out like a printer’s proof. The crop marks, registration targets and colour bar are the marks a press operator checks before a poster is trimmed. Here they are a reminder that this is a proof, not a finished business.

What is real.

  • The design, the code and the drawings
  • The form validation and the keyboard support
  • The network on the home page, which reacts to your pointer

What is not.

  • The agency, its team and its clients
  • Any result, figure or testimonial
  • The contact form, which sends nothing
